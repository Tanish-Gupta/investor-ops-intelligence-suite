About Edelweiss Flexi Cap Fund

Edelweiss Flexi Cap Fund Direct Growth is an equity fund. This fund was started on 3 February, 2015. The fund is managed by Ashwani Agarwalla, Mehul Dalmia, Trideep Bhattacharya. The fund could potentially beat inflation in the long-run.

Key Parameters

  1. Edelweiss Flexi Cap Fund has ₹3698 Cr worth of assets under management (AUM) as on Sep 2026 and is more than category average.
  2. The fund has an expense ratio 0.8.

Returns

Edelweiss Flexi Cap Fund has given a CAGR return of 13.45% since inception. Over the last 1, 3 and 5 years the fund has given a CAGR return of -0.10%, 13.25% and 11.87% respectively.

Holdings

Edelweiss Flexi Cap Fund has allocated its funds majorly in Cash Equivalent, Financial Services, Consumer Cyclical, Industrial, Tech, Basic Materials, Health, Consumer Defensive, Energy, Real Estate, Utilities, Communication, Corporate, Government. Its top holdings are HDFC Bank Ltd, Larsen & Toubro Ltd, Reliance Industries Ltd, State Bank of India, Tata Steel Ltd

Taxation

As it is a flexi cap mutual fund the taxation is as follows:
For short term (less than a year) capital gains will be taxed at 20%
For long term (more than 1 year) capital gains will be taxed at 12.5% without indexation benefit
Dividends will always be taxed at slab rate. Long term gains upto Rs 1 lakh are exempt capital gains tax.

Investment objective of Edelweiss Flexi Cap Fund

The investment objective of the Scheme is to generate long-term capital appreciation from a diversified portfolio that dynamically invests in equity and equity-related securities of companies across various market capitalisation. However, there can be no assurance that the investment objective of the Scheme will be realized.

Minimum Investment and lockin period

Minimum investment for lump sum payment is INR 100.00 and for SIP is INR 100.00. Edelweiss Flexi Cap Fund has no lock in period.
