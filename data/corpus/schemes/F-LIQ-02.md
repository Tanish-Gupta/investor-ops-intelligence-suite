**Edelweiss**

**Liquid Fund**

**An open-ended liquid scheme.**

**Investment Objective**

The investment objective of the Scheme is to provide reasonable returns, commensurate with low risk while providing a high level

of liquidity, through a portfolio of money market and debt securities. However there can be no assurance that the investment

objectives of the Scheme will be realized.
